Neurotome anatomical meshes
============================

The file brain.glb and the structure names in manifest.json are derived from
Z-Anatomy (built on BodyParts3D / DBCLS) and are distributed under
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).

© Z-Anatomy contributors and The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS),
via the Brain Project atlas assembly (itayinbarr/brainproject).

Some deep nuclei, thalamic groups, and white-matter tracts were registered
from open MNI-space atlases (CIT168, Najdenovska thalamic atlas, Neudorfer
hypothalamic atlas, HCP1065 tract templates). Those structures are approximate
(about 7 mm) and are for study, not for clinical use. They remain CC BY-SA 4.0.

You may share and adapt the model if you give attribution and distribute
derivatives under the same license.

The dural and arachnoid envelopes, the laminar tissue window, and the
laboratory interface are original schematic teaching geometry. They are not
a scan and not a substitute for an anatomical atlas in the operating room.
